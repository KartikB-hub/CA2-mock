You keep the notebook. What if everyone could see it?

You run a savings circle. When one payment is late, you do the chasing and you get the blame.

OurTurn is a prototype. One screen shows who has paid this month and whose turn is next.

Try the prototype

How it works

  1. One screen shows who has paid. All ten names for this month are on the first screen.
  2. You mark each payment. When €100 comes in, press "Received". The screen changes at once.
  3. The same screen shows whose turn is next. This month's name and next month's name are at the top.

Today the prototype works on one phone. Sharing it with the whole circle comes next.

What you should know
